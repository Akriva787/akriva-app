Akriva — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Akriva ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information is collected when you use our app at akriva787.github.io/akriva-app and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account details: Your name and mobile number when you sign in.
  • Order details: Delivery address, contact number and order items placed through the app.
  • Device data: Browser type, and basic usage information if collected through analytics.

2. How We Use Information

  • To process and deliver your orders.
  • To verify your identity via OTP when you log in.
  • To provide customer support and resolve issues.
  • To improve our products and shopping experience.

3. Data Storage

Data is stored securely and only retained as long as necessary for the purposes listed above. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us.

4. Payments

Payment details are processed through third-party payment providers (UPI, card, COD). We do not store your full card or UPI credentials.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

6. Third-Party Services

The app may use services like Google Fonts and hosted images. These third parties may collect limited data according to their own privacy policies.

7. Contact Us

For any privacy questions or requests to delete your data, email us at care@akriva.in.
